# Find the chapters

Action: `audiobook.plan`

## What it is for

Find the chapters of a long text and how long each takes to read aloud. Nothing is spoken.

## Steps in the app

1. Open **Voice › Audiobook**.
2. Paste the text; the chapters are listed.

## Fields

- `text`: The book.
- `target`: About how long a chapter is, when the text has no headings.

## Limits

- Up to 400,000 characters; 200 chapters.

## Needs

- Nothing.

## Examples

- How many chapters is this book?

## Problems

- **Too long** Split the text into two books.
