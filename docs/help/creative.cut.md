# Make a music video

Action: `creative.cut`

## What it is for

Make the video of a project's cut at full size: each piece's chosen picture or clip for its length, with its camera
move and fades, the song under it, and the captions and titles drawn on. It is kept with the project so you can watch
it; keeping it in Your videos is `creative.export`.

## Steps in the app

1. Open **Creative Studio**, open a project and go to **Cut**.
2. Choose **Make a version to watch**.
3. Watch it on the stage. After a change to the cut, it says the cut has changed since.

## Fields

- `project_id`: The project.

## Limits

- 1920 by 1080 for landscape, 1080 by 1920 for portrait and 1080 by 1080 for square, at 24 frames a second, H.264
  with AAC sound.
- On this Mac a 90-second song of stills took 19 seconds, so a 3-minute song takes about a minute; clips take longer.
- A piece with no picture yet, or whose picture was deleted, is black, and the screen says how many.
- Captions are drawn with a plain white font with an outline. Motion graphics templates are not drawn over the cut yet.
- Only one video, set of stills or preview is made at a time.

## Needs

- A project with at least one still.

## Examples

- Put the video together so I can watch it.

## Problems

- *There are no pictures in the cut yet.* Make the stills first, on the Shots step.
- *The song is not in Your music any more.* Choose another song in the brief.
