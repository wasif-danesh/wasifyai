# Preview a music video

Action: `creative.preview`

## What it is for

Join the chosen stills into a video over the song, with a camera move on each and cuts on the plan's times. It is
a fast draft to watch, not the finished video.

## Steps in the app

1. Open **Creative Studio**, open a project and go to **Shots**.
2. Choose **Preview the whole song**.
3. Watch it above the stills. After a change, it says the shots have changed since, and you can make it again.

## Fields

- `project_id`: The project.

## Limits

- It takes under a minute on a fast Mac and replaces the last preview.
- A shot with no still is black, and the screen says how many.
- No captions, and no singing or moving clips unless you added one to a shot. Those come with the Cut step.
- The preview is 1280 by 720 at 24 frames a second (or the same size for portrait and square).

## Needs

- A project with at least one still.

## Examples

- Show me the whole song with the pictures so far.

## Problems

- *The song is not in Your music any more.* The preview needs it. Choose another song in the brief.
