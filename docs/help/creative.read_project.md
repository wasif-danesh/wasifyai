# Read a plan

Action: `creative.read_project`

## What it is for

Read the plan of a project in Creative Studio: its idea, its cast, its parts, and every shot with its times, motion, camera, place and words. The assistant in the project's Ask tab works from this.

## Steps in the app

1. Open **Creative Studio** and open a project. The plan is on the screen.

## Fields

- `project_id`: The project.
- `shot_id`: The shot the person has selected, if any.

## Limits

- It only reads.

## Needs

- Nothing else.

## Examples

- "What happens in the second part?"

## Problems

- "That project is not in the library": it was deleted, or it belongs to someone else.
