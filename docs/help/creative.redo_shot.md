# Make a shot's still again

Action: `creative.redo_shot`

## What it is for

Make another still of one shot. The old one stays as a version you can go back to, and the new one is used.

## Steps in the app

1. Open **Creative Studio**, open a project and go to **Shots**.
2. Select a shot and choose **Make it again**.
3. Choose another version under the picture to go back to it.

Or ask in the **Ask** tab: select a shot and ask for another picture of it.

## Fields

- `project_id`: The project.
- `shot_id`: The shot.
- `mode`: `reference` (the default) or `plain`.

## Limits

- It takes under a minute on a fast Mac.
- Only one thing is made for a project at a time.

## Needs

- A project with a plan, and a picture model.

## Examples

- Make shot 4 again.
- I do not like this picture, try another.

## Problems

- *The same fault comes back.* Change the words of the shot first, then make it again.
