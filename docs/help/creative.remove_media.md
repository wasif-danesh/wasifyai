# Take out of a project

Action: `creative.remove_media`

## What it is for

Take a thing out of a project in Creative Studio. It stays in the Library.

## Steps in the app

1. Open the project in **Creative Studio**.
2. In the media rail, choose the cross on the thing.

## Fields

- `project_id`: The project.
- `kind`: What kind of thing it is.
- `item_id`: The thing in the Library.

## Limits

- The project's song cannot be taken out here. Change the song in the brief.

## Needs

- Nothing else.

## Examples

- Take the second picture out of this project.

## Problems

- "That project is not in the library": it was deleted, or it belongs to someone else.
