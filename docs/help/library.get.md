# Look at one creation

Action: `library.get`

## What it is for

Show what is known about one creation: its name, when it was made and its size or length.

## Steps in the app

1. Open **Library** and choose the creation.

## Fields

- `kind`: Which sort of creation.
- `id`: Its id, from a search or a list.

## Limits

- Only your own creations.

## Needs

- Nothing.

## Examples

- Tell me about that song.

## Problems

- **Not in the library** It was deleted, or the id is wrong. Search again.
