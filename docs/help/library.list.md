# List my creations

Action: `library.list`

## What it is for

Show the newest creations of one kind (pictures, videos, speech, songs, transcripts, voices, talking videos or notebooks).

## Steps in the app

1. Open **Library**. The screens' **See all** opens it showing their kind.
2. Choose the kind you want to see (each shows how many there are), and sort by newest, oldest or name.

## Fields

- `kind`: Which sort of creation.
- `limit`: How many, at most 50.

## Limits

- At most 50 at a time.

## Needs

- Nothing.

## Examples

- Show my last five pictures.

## Problems

- **The list is empty** Nothing of that kind has been made yet.
