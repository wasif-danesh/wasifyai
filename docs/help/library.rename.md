# Rename a creation

Action: `library.rename`

## What it is for

Give a song, a transcript, a voice, a notebook or a talking video a new name.

## Steps in the app

1. Open **Library** and choose the creation.
2. Edit its name and confirm.

## Fields

- `kind`: Which sort of creation.
- `id`: Its id.
- `title`: The new name, up to 200 characters.

## Limits

- Pictures, videos and speech have no name to change.

## Needs

- Nothing.

## Examples

- Rename that song to Rain song.

## Problems

- **Cannot be renamed** That kind of creation is named by what it says; it has no title.
