# Keep a report template

Action: `meetings.add_template`

## What it is for

Keep a report template of your own, in plain words: what the report should say. It is offered beside
the app's own templates for every meeting.

## Steps in the app

1. Open a meeting in **Meetings › View meetings**.
2. Under Reports, choose New template, give it a name and say what the report should contain, and
   choose Save.
3. Choose it and Write. Delete a template of your own from the same list.

## Fields

- `title`: The template's name.
- `instructions`: What the report says, in plain words.

## Limits

- Templates are yours; the app's own six cannot be changed.

## Needs

- Nothing more than a meeting to try it on.

## Examples

- Keep a template called Client update: what the client asked for, what we promised and by when.

## Problems

- **There is no such template** It was deleted.
