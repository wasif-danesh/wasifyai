# List the people of meetings

Action: `meetings.people`

## What it is for

See the people Meetings knows by voice: everyone you named as a speaker, with how many meetings they
were named in and how many voiceprints of them are kept. A speaker of a later meeting whose voice is
close to one of them is suggested as them.

## Steps in the app

1. Open **Meetings › People**.
2. Each person shows their meetings and voiceprints. Choose Rename to change a name everywhere, or
   Forget to delete them and every voiceprint of them.

## Fields

- No fields.

## Limits

- People are made only by naming a speaker; there is no other way to add one.
- Voiceprints stay on this computer.

## Needs

- The voiceprint model (about 26 MB, in Models) to keep voiceprints. Without it people are kept by
  name only.

## Examples

- Who do my meetings know?
- How many meetings was Ravi in?
