# Get a model

Action: `models.download`

## What it is for

Download a model that is missing. You are shown the files and their size and must agree.

## Steps in the app

1. Open **Model hub**.
2. Choose the model and read the size.
3. Choose Get and confirm.

## Fields

- `model_id`: The model, from the list.

## Limits

- One download at a time.
- Always needs your yes in the app window.
- A model that will not fit this computer is refused.

## Needs

- Free disk space.

## Examples

- Get the speech model.

## Problems

- **A download is already running** Wait for it, or cancel it in **Model hub**.
- **Will not fit** Choose a smaller model.
