# See your brand

Action: `motion.read_brand`

## What it is for

See the brand you set up for your videos: its name, colours, letters, call to action and web address.

## Steps in the app

1. Open a project in **Motion graphics**.
2. Under **Looks**, your brand is at the top.

## Fields

- Nothing to give.

## Limits

- There is one brand for each person.

## Needs

- Nothing else: Motion graphics must be set up to make the video.

## Examples

- "What is my call to action?"

## Problems

- If no brand is set up yet, it says so.
