# Read a video project

Action: `motion.read_project`

## What it is for

Read a video project: its shape, its length, and each scene's words, length and look. The assistant in the Motion graphics editor works from this.

## Steps in the app

1. Open the project in **Motion graphics**.

## Fields

- `composition_id`: The project.
- `scene_id`: The scene the person is on, if any.

## Limits

- It only reads.

## Needs

- Nothing else.

## Examples

- "What does this video say?"

## Problems

- **That video project does not exist** It may have been deleted; open Motion graphics to see what is left.
