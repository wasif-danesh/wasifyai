# Set up your brand

Action: `motion.set_brand`

## What it is for

Save your brand once: name, logo, four colours, letters, and the words of your end card. Every new video starts with it. Videos you have already made do not change, and a project already open keeps what it has until you use the brand again.

## Steps in the app

1. Open a project in **Motion graphics**.
2. Under **Looks**, choose **Set up your brand** (or **Change your brand**).
3. Add your logo, choose the colours and letters, and write your end card words.
4. Choose **Save your brand**.

## Fields

- `name`: The brand's name.
- `background`, `second`, `text`, `accent`: Colours, as #rrggbb.
- `font`: The letters.
- `action`: The end card's call to action.
- `address`: The end card's web address or place.
- `logo_on_scenes`: Whether the logo sits in the corner of every scene.

## Limits

- A logo is a PNG, JPEG or WebP picture, added in the app.
- The shipped letters cover Latin writing only today.

## Needs

- Nothing else: Motion graphics must be set up to make the video.

## Examples

- "Our brand colours are #102030 for the background and #ff8800 for the accent."

## Problems

- A colour that is not #rrggbb is refused, and nothing changes.
- The window warns when the words may be hard to read on your colours. It does not stop you.
