# Start from a marketing video

Action: `motion.start_from`

## What it is for

Start a new project from a set of scenes made for the job: a product promo, a launch, a sale or offer, customer words, a feature explainer, before and after, or a social post. Each ends with an end card, and every scene can be changed. The words are there for you to replace, and your brand is on it if you have one.

## Steps in the app

1. Open **Motion graphics**.
2. Under **Or start from**, choose a starting point and a shape.
3. Choose **Start from** to open it as it is, or describe your video first and choose **Make a first version**, and the assistant writes the words of those scenes.

## Fields

- `start`: promo, launch, offer, customer, explainer, before_after or social.
- `shape`: landscape, portrait, square or feed (4:5).
- `name`: The name of the project.

## Limits

- Up to 30 scenes and 90 seconds in all, as for any project.

## Needs

- Nothing else: Motion graphics must be set up to make the video.

## Examples

- "Start a launch announcement, square."

## Problems

- The video is not made yet: this only starts the project. Choose **Make the video** when the words are yours.
