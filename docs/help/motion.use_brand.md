# Use your brand on a video

Action: `motion.use_brand`

## What it is for

Put your brand on the project you have open, or take it off: its colours on every scene, its letters, and its logo in the corner. An end card still showing its first words takes the words of your brand. Words you typed stay. It is one step for Undo.

## Steps in the app

1. Open the project in **Motion graphics**.
2. Under **Looks**, tick **Use it in this video** (or clear it to take the brand off).

## Fields

- `composition_id`: The project.
- `use`: True puts the brand on, false takes it off.

## Limits

- While the brand is on, its colours replace the look of every scene.

## Needs

- Nothing else: Motion graphics must be set up to make the video.

## Examples

- "Use our brand colours."
- "Take the brand off this video."

## Problems

- If no brand is set up yet, it says to set one up first.
