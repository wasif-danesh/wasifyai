# Write lyrics

Action: `music.lyrics`

## What it is for

Write song lyrics from an idea. Nothing is saved; use them in Music › Create.

## Steps in the app

1. Open **Music › Lyrics**.
2. Say what the song is about and the style.
3. Choose **Write the lyrics**. The same writer is in **Music › Create** as **Write them for me** in the Lyrics
   panel.

## Fields

- `idea`: What the song is about.
- `style`: The kind of music.
- `language`: The language.

## Limits

- Idea of 3 to 500 characters.

## Needs

- The chat model.

## Examples

- A song about a lighthouse keeper, folk.

## Problems

- **Too short** Say a little more about the song.
