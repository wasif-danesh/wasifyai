# Search inside notebooks

Action: `notebook.search`

## What it is for

Find passages about a topic across all your notebooks.

## Steps in the app

1. Open **Notebook › View Notebooks** and search inside a notebook, or ask the assistant.

## Fields

- `query`: What to look for.

## Limits

- Up to 300 characters.

## Needs

- The notebooks' search model (Models).

## Examples

- What is the launch budget?

## Problems

- **Search model missing** Open **Models** and get the search model.
