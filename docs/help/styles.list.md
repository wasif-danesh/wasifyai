# Styles on this computer

Action: `styles.list`

## What it is for

See the styles (also called LoRAs) the image screens can use: the ones you made in Train, added from this computer
or got from Hugging Face, with what model each was made for and its word. Your saved looks are listed too.

## Steps in the app

1. Open **Images › Create**.
2. Under **Styles**, choose **Add a style** to see them.

## Fields

None.

## Limits

- Desktop only: the web shows the styles an admin installed.

## Needs

- The desktop app.

## Examples

- Which styles do I have?
- Use my anime style for this picture.

## Problems

- **Made for another model** That style cannot be used here; it would give noise.
- **Made for klein base** Its look may barely show on this model.
