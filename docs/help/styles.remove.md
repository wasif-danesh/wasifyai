# Delete a style

Action: `styles.remove`

## What it is for

Delete the app's copy of a style from this computer. Pictures made with it stay. A file another app keeps is never
touched.

## Steps in the app

1. Open **Images › Create** and choose the style under **Styles**.
2. In its menu, choose **Delete from this computer…** and confirm.

## Fields

- `style_id`: The style, from the list.

## Limits

- Always asks you first.

## Needs

- The desktop app.

## Examples

- Delete the yarn style.

## Problems

- **Not on this computer** It was deleted already.
