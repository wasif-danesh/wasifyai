# Change a voice

Action: `voice.convert`

## What it is for

Make a recording sound like one of your copied voices. The original stays.

## Steps in the app

1. Open **Voice › Change a voice**.
2. Choose the recording and the voice.
3. Choose **Change the voice**. Under a speech you made, **Change the voice** opens this screen with it
   chosen.

## Fields

- `source_kind`: track or speech.
- `source_id`: The recording.
- `saved_voice`: A copied voice.

## Limits

- The voice must be one copied from a recording.

## Needs

- A voice-changing model (Models).

## Examples

- Make this song sound like my voice.

## Problems

- **A described voice cannot be used** Choose a voice copied from a recording.
