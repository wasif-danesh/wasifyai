# Describe a voice

Action: `voice.design`

## What it is for

Save a voice described in words (age, tone, pace) to use again. No recording is needed.

## Steps in the app

1. Open **Voice › Voices**, or choose **Make a voice** under the voice list in **Voice › Text to speech**.
2. Choose to describe a voice.
3. Give it a name and a description.
4. Save.

## Fields

- `name`: A name.
- `description`: The voice in words, up to 300 characters, without brackets.

## Limits

- 300 characters; no brackets.

## Needs

- Nothing to save it; a model that can use it to speak.

## Examples

- Calm narrator: a calm middle-aged woman, slow and warm.

## Problems

- **Brackets** Take the brackets out of the description.
