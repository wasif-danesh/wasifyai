# My saved voices

Action: `voice.list`

## What it is for

List the voices you saved, so you can use one by name or id.

## Steps in the app

1. Open **Voice › Voices**.

## Fields

- `kind`: Optional: design (described) or clone (copied).

## Limits

- Nothing.

## Needs

- Nothing.

## Examples

- Which voices do I have?

## Problems

- **No voices** Describe or copy one (`voice.design`, `voice.clone`).
