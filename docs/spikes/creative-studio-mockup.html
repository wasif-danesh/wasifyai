What this borrows from Maestro: a tool column on the left that makes things, a large preview in the middle, a rail of everything made on the right, filters above the preview, and the honest line above Generate. It does not copy any of its code or wording.
What is ours: one project that holds the plan, the shots and the cut (the Plan, Shots and Cut buttons), the song and the captions on the timeline, and every product of the app in the left column: Image, Video, Voice, Music, Titles (Motion graphics) and 3D. Whatever is made there lands in the project's media rail and can be dropped on the timeline. The model button for the active tab is at the left of the top bar, as on every screen.
Honest limits: pictures are sketches made of colour, not real output; the times shown are examples in the style of our measured ones (a 2 s clip is about 72 s today).
