What this borrows from Unsloth's image screen: a list of up to eight adapters, each with a strength from 0 to 2, an "Add" button, a way to look in other folders, and suggestions for the loaded model. It does not copy its code or wording.
What is ours: styles you trained in the app come first and are marked; each one says in words whether it fits the model (read from the file, not guessed from the name); a trigger word has a "Use this word" button; downloads ask first and show the size; only .safetensors files are accepted; and the honest line says what the styles cost in time.
Honest limits: the pictures are sketches. The numbers are the ones S0 measured (docs/spikes/styles-s0.md). Amended 2026-10-30: licence lines removed (ADR-0094), the time line from S0.
