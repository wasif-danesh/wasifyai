Motion graphics editor: mockup. Pictures are sketches drawn for this page, not real output. Nothing saves or renders.

What to try

  • Assistant tab: press the suggestions. Each change lands in the preview and the scene strip and can be undone from its own card. Making a voiceover says what it costs first.
  • Arrange: drag the words and the can on the preview, drag a corner to resize, or use the arrow keys on a focused item.
  • Scene strip: pick a scene, drag to reorder, or use the arrows. Change the shape in the top bar. Press play.
  • Add panel: Media, Words, Sound, Looks, Make (the other products) and Brand.
  • Make the video opens the export dialog, with several shapes at once. New project is the first screen.

Questions for the owner

  1. The assistant shares the right column with the scene's details, as two tabs. This keeps the preview large. The other way is a fourth column, which leaves the preview small on a laptop. Recommended: two tabs.
  2. Make sits among the Add tools, not as a screen of its own, so a generated picture goes straight into the scene. Recommended: yes.
  3. The story strip is scene cards, not a timeline with tracks. This is KinoFold's way and the plan's. Recommended: yes for now.
  4. The first screen asks what to promote, and the five one-off graphics (title, quote, chart, name strap, captions) stay as a row under it. Recommended: yes.
  5. Anything to change in the regions, tabs or words? Say what, and the mockup changes before any code.
