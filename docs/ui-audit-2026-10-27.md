# UI audit, 2026-10-27

Every product screen of Wasify AI, looked at as a person who wants to make something, not as the people who built
it. The plan that follows from this audit is `docs/ui-improvement-plan.md`; the decisions it needs are ADR-0093
(Proposed).

## The pictures

Two sets, because the app has two faces that are meant to be one (ADR-0087):

- **`docs/ui-audit/desktop/`: the real desktop window** (64 pictures), with the real engine and this computer's
  own data: the saved chats, the 21 creations, the one notebook, the taught model "Ena", the models that are
  installed. Every screen at 1440 by 900, the long ones again in a tall window (`-tall`), ten of them in dark
  mode (`-dark`), plus the states a route cannot reach: the model picker on three screens (`picker-*`), the side

The window pictures were taken again after stage U0 of the plan was built the same day, so `models*`,

## The short version

The app works, and a lot of it is thoughtful: the plain words, the consent lines, the sentences that say what
leaves the computer, the three-pane notebook, the chat, the Train front door, the time estimates. It does not feel
like one product, and the window and the web do not feel like the same product either. Four things cost the most:

1. **Three page layouts for the same job.** Images is a studio (controls on the left, the result in the middle,
   a strip of recent pictures): the right shape. Video is a wide white card on a grey page. Every voice and music
   screen is a bare form in a narrow column under a heading and a paragraph. A person who learns one screen has
   learnt nothing about the next.
2. **The main action has nine names** ("Generate", "Make my video", "Speak", "Make", "Make the video", "Write the
   lyrics", "Write the script", "Find the chapters", "Fill in the form", "Change the voice", "Save voice"), and the
   expert fields are on the first screen on some tools (Speed, Seed, Tempo, Key, Takes) and hidden on others. The
   design guide says "make", not "generate", and "For experts" for the rest.
3. **Five libraries, and two of them are called Library.** "Library" in the menu is My creations; "Your images"
   opens a page whose heading is also "Library"; "Your videos", "Your music", and the "Made before", "Earlier",
   "Your videos", "Your 3D models", "Your videos" (Motion graphics) lists inside eight tools, each with its own
   search or none. The magnifier in the side menu says "Search My creations" and opens Your images.
4. **The window and the web have different shells.** The window: back and forward, the menu toggle, the model
   button or a "Group: Item" title in the bar, no search box, no theme switch, no sign of a running job. The web:
   a breadcrumb, "Search everything… ⌘K", a job chip, a theme toggle, and an "Explore" page the window does not
   have. ⌘K does nothing in the window.

Besides those: the model picker opens over the form it belongs to; the Models table clips its "Use" button and
wraps "Licence not reviewed" into four lines; a track in Your music carries eleven text actions in a row; Settings
is one page of nine unrelated sections; two web screens show a raw endpoint path in an error; and the 3D screen
says "It ismade on this computer".

## How the app is put together today

- **Shell (window).** A 36 px bar: back, forward, hide the menu, then either the screen's model button (Images,
  Video, Speech, Music, Lip sync, 3D, the chat) or a title such as "Voice: Dub a recording" or "Notebook: View
  notebooks"; on Images, Create and Library tabs at the right; on a notebook, its name and "3 sources". A side menu
  (brand, a magnifier, New chat, five groups that open one at a time, then Motion graphics, 3D model, Library, Train
  models, Model hub, Recents, and the person with a Settings cog). Dark mode follows the system; there is no switch.
- **Shell (web).** The same menu plus Explore, a top bar with the menu toggle, a breadcrumb ("Voice › Text to
  speech"), "Search everything… ⌘K", a job chip ("Making your video…"), a theme toggle; the model button in a second
  bar or in the page.
- **Screens.** 31 in the window: chat (new and saved), My creations, Images (Create, Edit, Inpaint, Reference, Your
  images, 3D), Video (Create, Animate, Extend, Motion graphics, Your videos), Voice (Text to speech, Speech to text,
  Voices, Change a voice, Edit speech, Dub, Audiobook, Lip sync), Music (Create, Your music, Lyrics, Split into
  parts), Notebooks (list, notebook, new notebook), Train (the front door and three flows), Model hub, Settings. The
  web has all but Settings and Model hub, and adds Explore.

## Screen by screen

The steps count what a person does from arriving on the screen to having the thing, with a model already set
up. "Compare" names the app people would measure this against. Pictures are named by their file in
`docs/ui-audit/desktop/` unless marked (web).

### Home (chat) — `home`, `home-dark`, `chat-saved`, `chat-servers`, `picker-chat`

Wants: ask for something, or get to a tool. Today: the model button in the bar, "Good evening" with a decorative
"w" medallion, one box with six pills (Attach, Thinking, Search, Asks first, Servers, Mic), three suggestions.
Steps to a picture from here: type, send, read the card, Go ahead: four; the picture appears in the chat with
"Open in the library". A saved chat (`chat-saved`) reads well: the answer, a "Thinking" row with Show, copy,
regenerate and read-aloud icons under each answer.

- Good: calm; one box; the suggestion chips; the card that asks before making; the answers' typography.
- Unclear: "Asks first" is a mode name nobody would guess (it is the approval setting); six pills of equal weight
  for things a person touches once a month (Servers opens a notice that says no server is connected, with a Close
  button: a dead end that should not be a pill); the model button shows a raw tag with no readiness; nothing says
  what the app can make, so a new person types "hi".
- Missing: quick starts for the products; a way to see what is running; the person's name instead of "You".
- Compare: Claude Desktop and ChatGPT keep two or three tools in the box and put the rest in a "+" menu; Midjourney's
  web app puts a prompt bar over a wall of what everyone made.

### Images: Create, Edit, Inpaint, Reference, Your images — `image-*`, `picker-images`, `menu-hidden`

Wants: a picture from words, or a change to a picture. Today: the model button at the left of the bar and
Create / Library tabs at its right; a left column (heading, prompt, "Press Ctrl or ⌘ + Enter to generate.", Improve
prompt, 0/500, three chips, Aspect ratio, "Styles (optional) +"), "Generate" fixed at the bottom, the result area
with "Your image appears here", and a strip of ten recent pictures. Steps: type, Generate: two. Edit, Inpaint and
Reference add a picture drop zone above the prompt.

- Good: this is the right layout; the drop zone; the strip of real pictures; the fixed main button; it survives a
  hidden menu (`menu-hidden`).
- Unclear: "Generate"; the four tools are four menu items and the bar also has a "Create" tab, so the person has
  two ways to the same place and "Library" there is Your images, not the Library; the strip has no title and no
  "see all"; Styles is a collapsed "+" a person will not find (ADR-0091 wants it visible); the picker opens over
  the prompt (`picker-images`).
- Missing: progress inside the result area (there is none, and the window has no chip); a history that scrolls;
  actions on the result (edit this, animate this, use as reference, save, delete) are icons that only appear after
  a make; a compare with the previous result.
- Compare: Midjourney and Ideogram show the prompt bar and the gallery as one surface and every picture carries
  its actions on hover; Runway puts the tools as tabs over one canvas.

### Video: Create, Animate a picture, Extend, Your videos, Motion graphics — `video-*`

Wants: a short clip from words or a picture. Today: the model button in the bar; a white card holds the form
(What should the video show?, a hint with the keyboard shortcut, Improve prompt, chips, Length with "a few minutes"
and "takes about twice as long", Shape) and "Make my video", then a sentence about how long it takes. Steps: type,
Make my video: two; then wait several minutes with nothing on the screen. Extend (`video-extend`) puts a grid of
every clip, a drop button and a prompt inside one card, and the Make button is below the fold.

- Good: the time given in words beside each length; the shapes; Extend's grid of real clips with their lengths.
- Unclear: why Video is a card when Images is a studio; where the video will appear (no result area); the web's
  "You can explore the site while it's made".
- Missing: the result area and progress where the person is; the recent clips on Create; a way from a finished
  clip to Extend or to Animate (backlog D17); on the web, Motion graphics and 3D show two raw errors.
- Compare: Runway and Pika keep the prompt at the bottom and the stage above, with every clip in a strip.

Motion graphics (`video-builder-tall`) is its own product: "Your videos" in a left column, a large serif "What
should we make?", a prompt box with Attach a CSV and three shape chips, "Fill in the form →", five example cards, "Or
fill in the form yourself". It is a good flow in a look no other screen shares.

### Voice: Text to speech, Speech to text, Voices, Change a voice, Edit speech, Dub, Audiobook, Lip sync — `voice-*`, `picker-speech`

Wants: hear words in a voice; words from a recording; a voice of their own; a recording in another language; a
book read; a face that speaks. Today: eight screens in one narrow column each: heading, a paragraph (two on Dub,
Audiobook and Change a voice), the form, one button, "Made before" / "Earlier" / "Your videos" under it. Text to
speech has a native select of 28 built-in voices, a Speed slider, a Seed box, a two-line text box, "Speak", and a
"Made before" card with a native player, Save and Delete. Voices has a well-made Describe or Clone switch. Dub and
Audiobook are numbered steps that reveal one at a time. Lip sync is a long form: Choose a picture, three consent
radios, Words to say or A recording, a notice about the AI-generated mark, "Make the video".

- Good: the consent lines; the numbered steps; the time estimate on Lip sync ("About 1 minutes of work on this
  computer for 0:02 of video": the plural is wrong); the honest sentences about what leaves the computer.
- Unclear: the voice is chosen from a native select with no way to hear it; the person's own voices (from Voices)
  are not in the same control; Speed and Seed are on the first screen; the web's "Model for …" placeholder;
  "Made before" versus Your music versus Library; Change a voice has two native selects and "No saved voices yet"
  as an option.
- Missing: a player and a waveform where the result lands; a voices panel (pick a voice by hearing it); a text box
  sized for a tool whose input is text; history with play, download, use again; progress for a long audiobook;
  keyboard shortcuts.
- Compare: ElevenLabs' studio: text on the left, voice and settings on the right, every take listed under the text
  with play and download, the voice library a panel with previews.

### Music: Create, Your music, Lyrics, Split into parts — `music-*`

Wants: a song. Today (window, `music-create-tall`): one long form: Describe the music, Lyrics with "Write lyrics for
me", No singing, Length slider, Tempo, Key, Seed, then "Make · Takes [1] · About 19 seconds of work on this
computer", then "Made before" with a card that has a native player and seven text actions. On the web without a
model the screen is a dead end (a disabled Make and "Takes 1"). Lyrics is a one-line box, a language box and a
button. Split is a native select and a button. Your music (`music-tracks`) is one card per track with eleven
actions.

- Good: the lyrics hand off to Create; "About 19 seconds of work on this computer"; the Length slider in words.
- Unclear: Tempo, Key and Seed on the first screen; "Takes" is a word without a label; where songs go ("Your
  music" says "Everything you made" but holds only audio); eleven actions on a track.
- Missing: style chips; a player with the lyrics; a feed of what was made; one library.
- Compare: Suno: one panel (description, style, lyrics, instrumental), a feed of songs with players on the right,
  three actions on a song and the rest in a menu.

### Notebooks — `notebooks`, `notebook`, `notebook-new`, `notebook-dark`

Wants: ask questions about their own documents and make things from them. Today: a list of cards (name, sources,
when) and "New notebook"; a notebook is three panes (sources with checkboxes, the chat with citation pills and
"Save as note", Studio with eleven output cards and an Outputs list), the bar shows the notebook's name and "3
sources". A new notebook (`notebook-new`) has a good start card: Upload files, Copied text, Websites (Soon), a drop
zone, and the Studio greyed with "Add a source first."

- Good: the three panes; the citation pills; the Studio; the new-notebook card; the menu steps aside on a narrow
  window.
- Unclear: "Notebook: View notebooks" as a title; eleven equal Studio cards with no order of use; the Outputs
  list is text only; the notebook is "Untitled notebook" with no way to rename it in place.
- Missing: outputs as cards with a thumbnail; a notebook's own search; renaming in place; a way to open a source.
- Compare: NotebookLM: the same three panes, with the Studio's outputs as rich cards and a guided empty state.

### Library: My creations, Your images, Your videos, Your music — `creations*`, `image-library`, `video-library`, `music-tracks`, `search-sidebar`

Wants: find and reuse a thing they made. Today: My creations has a search box with a hint, kind chips with counts
(All 21, Music 1, Images 12, Videos 6, Speech 1, Transcripts 1, Lip syncs 0), a grid or table switch, cards with a
kind badge, the prompt as the title, when, and a state chip. Your images is a grid with another search, All and
Favourites, and the heading "Library". Your videos is a grid with the same search. Your music is a list with name
search and Favourites only.

- Good: My creations is the right idea; the kind badges; search in any language; the real pictures look good in
  the grid.
- Unclear: five places; the heading "Library" on Your images; the magnifier in the menu opens Your images
  although it says "Search My creations"; a card's title is the prompt, which is often long or a file name
  ("Hello_ how are you_ (1)").
- Missing: opening a creation (a detail view with the recipe, the actions and the related items); multi-select;
  sorting; the kinds that are not there (notebook outputs, 3D models, motion graphics, taught models).
- Compare: Midjourney's and ElevenLabs' history: one list, every item opens, every item carries its actions.

### Train, 3D model, Motion graphics — `train*`, `image-3d-tall`, `video-builder-tall`

Wants: teach the app, or a 3D model, or a title card. Today: Train is four cards with an eyebrow ("IMAGE MODEL
TRAINING"), a title, a sentence and Start (the video one disabled, with a "Set up video training" card below that
says what is downloaded and why, 183 MB), then "Sets of pictures and clips", "My models" (Ena, magic word
`gabrin`, "Not checked yet.", Rename, Remove, "Check and try it"), "Training tools". Start opens a flow with a
stepper (1 Add pictures, 2 Check, 3 Teach), a drop zone, a disabled Next with "Add at least 8 pictures (8 more)."
and a "Back to Train models" link. 3D is a card with "Start from: A picture, Words, Four photos (soon)", a drop
zone, "Your 3D models" with one card, and a licence line.

- Good: Train's front door and its honesty about what is downloaded; the stepper; the drop zones; "Experimental".
- Unclear: four cards of the same weight whether or not they work here; the disabled Start with its reason 300 px
  below; "Back to Train models" as a text link under the card instead of in the bar; the raw error on the web.
- Missing: the states a long job needs (a run's page with progress, pause, what it costs); the taught models in
  the Library.

### Model hub and the picker — `models*`, `picker-*`

Wants: pick a model, or get one. Today: tabs per job (Chat, Images, Video, Voice, Listening, Music, Lip sync,
Documents, 3D), a table (Model, Size, Licence, Status) with In use, On this computer and Not downloaded chips, a
"Use" button that is clipped at the right edge, a found model listed by its hash, and Advanced: every model as a
card with its files and paths, "Get the model", "Remove from the app's folder", then found files and the Hugging
Face search (`models-advanced`, 3200 px tall). The picker is a popover under the bar button with Recommended / On
this computer (/ More) and a list.

- Good: one tab per job; the memory line ("69 GB of memory, 110 GB free"); the "Fits · needs 24.0 GB" chips; the
  licence sentences.
- Unclear: the Licence column wrapping to four lines; the hash as a name; the "Use" button off the edge; the
  picker covering the form; raw names in the chat picker.
- Missing: a readiness dot (loaded, loading, needs a download) on the button and in the table; the same table in
  the picker's "More".

### Settings — `settings-tall`, `settings-dark`

One page, nine sections: Image generation, Language models, Finding models (a checkbox, a monospace textarea,
Save), Browsing models, Hugging Face token, Searching the web (five radios, a checkbox), Other apps (a checkbox,
Connections with New connection, Tools from other apps with Add a server and a Kind select), Updates, The engine.

- Good: every section says what leaves the computer; the engine's Restart and Repair are plain.
- Unclear: no grouping or index for nine unrelated things; a Save button in one section and none in the others;
  native controls; the MCP sections are long and technical beside "Updates".
- Missing: General, Models, Privacy and sharing, Other apps, Advanced as groups; search within settings; a "what
  this computer can do" summary; the person's name; the theme.

### Dark mode — `*-dark`

Works everywhere that was looked at. The side menu and the content are the same tone, so the menu's edge is a
hairline; the chat box and the cards sit well; the native selects and players stay light-looking.

### Phone (web, 390 px) — `*-phone` (web)

The home and Images work: the menu becomes a drawer, the main button is fixed, chips wrap. The top bar loses the
title and the "Making your video…" chip takes most of it.

## What is good and must stay

The plain words and sentence case; the consent and "leaves this computer" lines; the time estimates in words; the
card before a make in chat; the three-pane notebook with citations and its start card; the Images studio layout;
the state chips on creations; search in any language; the Train front door; dark mode; the fixed main button on a
phone; the menu stepping aside for a notebook.
